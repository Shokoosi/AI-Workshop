Mathematical Modeling

Linear Regression & Drift ($y = mx + b$)

Why Straight Lines Matter

In your AI prompts, mandate tension: 0 in Chart.js. Without this, charting libraries apply curved Bézier splines that artificially distort linear forecasts into waves or create false downturns at endpoints.

Equation: y = m · x + b
m = Monthly Drift Rate ($/month)
b = Estimated Baseline Value ($)

Core Metrics Every Group Must Report

  • • Slope ($m$): How many dollars per month did your portfolio gain or lose on average?
  • • R-Squared ($R^2$): Goodness-of-fit. Does the trendline explain the variation, or is it random volatile noise?
  • • Max Drawdown (%): The worst peak-to-trough crash your portfolio experienced during the historical year.

Prompt Engineering

The Hackathon Starter Prompt

I have attached an Excel file containing historical monthly prices for the S&P 500 and Bitcoin.

Please read the file and build a single-file interactive web dashboard using HTML, Tailwind CSS, and Chart.js to evaluate a $1,000 investment over a 24-month horizon (last 12 months history + next 12 months linear regression forecast).

Requirements:
1. Data Processing: Locate Date, S&P 500 price, and Bitcoin price. Extract the last 13 monthly points (Month 0 to Month 12). Normalize both to start at $1,000 at Month 0: Normalized(t) = 1000 * (Price(t) / Price(0)).
2. Interactive Slider: Add an allocation slider (0% to 100%) for S&P 500 allocation with remaining in Bitcoin. Include preset buttons (100% S&P, 75/25, 50/50, 25/75, 100% BTC).
3. Math & Regression: Fit an Ordinary Least Squares regression line (y = mx + b) over historical points. Forecast Months 13 through 24 using the formula. Calculate slope ($/month), R², and max drawdown.
4. Chart.js Specification: Set tension: 0 on all datasets to ensure the regression forecast is laser-straight. Solid line for history (Months 0-12), dashed line for forecast (Months 12-24). Add a vertical divider at Month 12.
5. KPI Cards: Display Month 12 Actual ($), Month 24 Forecast ($), Monthly Drift Slope ($/mo), and Max Drawdown (%).
6. Return a single runnable index.html file.
Instructor Guidance & Pro Recommendations
Moving Averages: Feel free to calculate a 3-month or 6-month simple or exponential moving average (SMA/EMA) to smooth out extreme crypto spikes and dips.
Seasonality & Cycles: Look for seasonality patterns (e.g., Q4 rallies, "Uptober", or post-halving crypto cycle behaviors) across historical months.
Volatility Bands: Try plotting ±1 Standard Deviation bands around your regression trendline to visualize upside and downside surprise channels.
Metric Depth: Prompt your AI to compute the Sharpe ratio, max drawdown recovery months, or downside risk capture ratios.
Evaluation

5-Minute Pitch Rubric (100 Points)

1. Visual Proof & Chart Precision 25 pts

3. 12-Month Horizon Justification 25 pts

Did they defend their allocation recommendation based on the strict 12-month exit constraint, addressing downside crash risk?

4. Innovation & Analytical Depth 25 pts

Bonus for moving averages, seasonal cyclicality, uncertainty envelopes, or creative interactive elements prompted with AI.
